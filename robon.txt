ROBON

The Pizza Blue Incident

Chapter 1. The Last Three Weeks

2014 — United States

Pizza Blue was a large family pizzeria and entertainment restaurant visited by both children and adults. It offered everything for fun: pizza, fast food, desserts, arcade games, ball pits, slides, and many play areas for children.

But far away from this place, deep in a forest, lived an unusual bear.

His name was MrBon.

He was extremely hungry. He had not eaten anything for three weeks and had been wandering through the forest, searching everywhere for food. But he found nothing.

After those three weeks, MrBon was in Chechnya. He noticed several airplanes nearby. Without thinking twice, he climbed inside one of them.

The airplane began moving.

Soon, it took off and headed toward the United States of America.

However, nobody knew that an enormous bear was inside.

During the flight, the airplane could not withstand MrBon's enormous weight. The baggage compartment opened.

MrBon fell from an incredible height.

He landed in a forest.

Surprisingly, he did not feel much pain from the fall. But one problem remained: he still needed to find food.

Chapter 2. The Smell of Pizza

MrBon continued searching for food. The forest seemed endless, and there was nothing suitable to eat.

Then, suddenly...

He smelled something.

The delicious smell of pizza.

MrBon immediately raised his head. His nose pointed toward the aroma, and he began running as fast as he could.

Soon, he discovered a large building.

Pizza Blue.

The bear approached the wall, broke a small hole in it, and squeezed his way inside.

There, he found freshly prepared pizza.

He started eating.

One pizza. Two pizzas. Three pizzas...

Eventually, he ate twenty whole pizzas.

But even that was not enough.

Suddenly, he smelled pizza again and immediately ran to find more food.

That was when something unexpected happened.

A little girl noticed MrBon. She approached him, liked how cute he looked, and immediately hugged him.

The bear did not understand what was happening.

But he had no intention of attacking her.

Other customers noticed him too. They liked the unusual bear and started feeding him and carefully petting his fur.

For the first time in a long while, MrBon was surrounded by people who actually liked him.

Chapter 3. The Director's Business

The director of Pizza Blue quickly noticed what was happening.

Customers were coming specifically to see MrBon. People wanted to meet him, feed him, and take pictures with him.

The director decided to turn the bear into the restaurant's main attraction.

It became an incredibly profitable idea.

Pizza Blue was now earning approximately $21,000 per month.

However, after two months, everything changed.

MrBon had become tired of eating pizza. He had lost his appetite and decided that the best option was to leave the restaurant.

One day, the director returned with another pizza.

But MrBon was nowhere to be found.

He searched the kitchen, the bathrooms, the attic, and every other room.

Nothing.

The director began to panic. Customers were expected to arrive the very next day. He was afraid of losing all his income.

Then his eyes noticed a toy robot.

Suddenly, he had an idea.

Chapter 4. The New Bear

The director invited a professional mechanic.

He asked him to build a mechanical copy of MrBon.

The work was difficult. There were problems with the robot's shape and appearance, but the director decided that it would be good enough.

And so, a new bear was created.

His name was Robon.

The next day, customers arrived to see the replacement.

But their reaction was completely unexpected.

They looked at the metallic bear with disappointment.

It was nothing more than a strange, smelly metal machine. It did not look like the real MrBon.

The original MrBon had sharp fur, and customers carefully petted him.

Robon was completely different.

The customers disliked the replacement and started leaving.

The director found himself in serious trouble again.

However, the restaurant continued operating.

Chapter 5. Andrey Indolidge

One day, a man named Andrey Indolidge arrived at Pizza Blue.

He noticed that Robon's back was open.

Curious about the robot's internal mechanisms, he decided to climb inside.

Eventually, he managed to enter Robon's body.

However, the interior was extremely hot. There were many mechanical components, and the robot was incredibly heavy.

Andrey could not hold on for long.

At one point, he accidentally fell headfirst and damaged the main chip.

Something unexpected happened.

The damaged chip accidentally created a virus capable of controlling everything.

But something even stranger occurred.

The chip accidentally became stuck in Andrey Indolidge's brain.

He began losing his mind and eventually lost consciousness.

He could no longer wake up or control anything himself.

From that moment on, only the virus was in control.

Chapter 6. The Final Show

The day of another show finally arrived.

Customers gathered inside Pizza Blue, expecting to see Robon.

But as soon as the show began, everyone realized something was terribly wrong.

Robon was not behaving normally.

Electrical sparks appeared everywhere. The robot screamed like a madman, and blood began appearing throughout the restaurant.

Suddenly, Robon fell again.

The electricity caused a fire.

A massive fire broke out.

Flames spread throughout the restaurant as customers desperately searched for an exit.

But the doors were blocked by enormous rocks.

All the shutters were closed. The buttons were not working. The other exits were also locked because the director had blocked the doors.

Everyone was trapped.

And then Robon began attacking.

Chapter 7. The Last Survivor

One hour passed.

The restaurant had become a terrible disaster scene. Dead people and children were everywhere. Blood covered the floor and Robon's hands.

But he continued searching for anyone who was still alive.

Fortunately, one boy had survived.

He knew he had to escape immediately.

Carefully moving through the restaurant, the boy entered a room.

But he accidentally made a noise.

Robon stopped.

He heard it.

He slowly turned and began moving toward the room.

The boy panicked and quickly closed the door.

He did not know what to do.

Then he looked up.

There was an open passage in the roof.

It was his only chance.

Inside the room, he found three things: gas, a rope, and a lighter.

An idea began forming in his mind.

The boy attached the rope to the gas canister and started climbing toward the opening in the roof.

But Robon was already approaching.

He struck the door with enormous force.

Another hit.

The door began breaking apart.

Finally, it burst open.

Gas was already escaping from the canister.

Robon looked up and noticed the boy.

The boy ignited the lighter and let it go.

Then he quickly ran away.

A massive explosion followed.

Everything shook.

The boy fell to the ground. He was still alive, but after the powerful explosion, he could no longer hear anything.

He had lost his hearing.

He had become deaf.

Robon was trapped beneath enormous rocks.

It seemed like everything was over.

But it wasn't.

Robon was still alive.

Chapter 8. The Return

2015 — One Year Later

One year had passed.

Deep beneath the rubble, Robon finally awakened.

He tried to move.

But his body was almost completely destroyed.

He had lost one arm.

He had lost one eye.

Both of his legs were gone.

The damaged chip was still functioning inside him.

His main goal was now to repair his body as quickly as possible. Otherwise, the remaining mechanical parts could become damaged too.

The chip began analyzing the environment.

Unexpectedly, it discovered that electrical cables could be used to control mechanical parts.

Robon started searching for cables.

He connected every cable he could find to his body.

Gradually, his hands began moving again.

But a new problem appeared.

There was not enough electricity.

He continued searching for useful equipment and eventually discovered a desktop computer.

Robon opened it, removed the battery, and connected it to his mechanical heart.

The electricity started working again.

Now his hand could move much better.

Then he found approximately one thousand cables and connected them to his body to restore his ability to walk.

But the load was too much.

His hand could not withstand it and became severely damaged.

Robon continued searching for replacement parts.

Finally, he discovered another mechanical hand.

It had the same shape, but a different color.

He had no other choice.

He installed the new hand.

Now he had a new working hand.

Robon continued his recovery.

But what will happen next?

Unknown...

The story continues.
