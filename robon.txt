# ROBON — The Pizza Blue Incident

The origin story of Robon.

## 2014 — Pizza Blue

In America there was a popular family pizzeria called Pizza Blue. It was a huge entertainment place for children: pizza, fast food, desserts, arcade machines, colorful balloons and big slides.

But one day an unusual guest arrived — MrBon.

He had lived in a forest for a long time, couldn't find any food, and was so hungry that he hadn't eaten anything for several weeks. After a long search he ended up in Chechnya, where he sneaked onto a plane heading to the USA.

During the flight, because of his enormous weight, the luggage compartment opened. MrBon fell from a great height and landed in a forest. Despite the fall, he survived and kept looking for food.

One day he smelled pizza. He ran toward the smell, broke a small hole in the wall of Pizza Blue and climbed inside.

There was ready-made pizza. MrBon began eating one after another. He was so hungry that he ate twenty pizzas.

Soon a little girl noticed him. She walked up to him and hugged him. MrBon didn't understand what was happening, but he didn't intend to hurt anyone.

The visitors liked him a lot. People began bringing him food and petting him.

## The Director's New Business

The director of Pizza Blue noticed the huge interest of the visitors in the bear and decided to turn him into the main attraction of the restaurant.

This turned out to be a very profitable decision: the restaurant started earning about $21,000 a month.

But two months later MrBon got tired of eating pizza all the time and decided to leave.

When the director came back, he found that the bear had disappeared.

He searched everywhere, but without success. There was very little time left before the next show.

So the director came up with a replacement — a mechanical bear.

He invited a professional mechanic, who built a robot named Robon.

## Robon's Failure

When the visitors came to see the new bear, they were disappointed.

Robon looked like a strange metal construction. He was heavy, smelled unpleasantly of metal, and didn't resemble the soft plush MrBon at all.

The visitors stopped coming.

The director's business began to fall apart.

## The Chip Accident

One day a man named Andrey Indolidge came to the restaurant.

He noticed the open back of Robon and decided to climb inside.

But it was too hot inside and the mechanical parts were very heavy. Andrey accidentally fell and damaged the main chip.

After the accident, the chip created an unknown virus.

It began to control Robon's system, and then accidentally got stuck in Andrey's brain.

Andrey lost consciousness and could no longer control his own body. Control passed to the virus.

## The Pizza Blue Catastrophe

During the next show, Robon began to behave completely abnormally.

Sparks, electricity and blood appeared everywhere. He screamed and moved uncontrollably.

After another accident, a fire broke out.

The doors were blocked, the shutters closed, and the visitors couldn't get out of the restaurant.

Robon began attacking people.

Within an hour the restaurant turned into the site of a real catastrophe. Most of the visitors died.

But one boy managed to survive.

## The Escape

The boy heard Robon's footsteps getting closer and hid in a room.

He noticed an open passage in the roof. Using items he found in the room, he prepared a way to get outside.

Robon broke down the door and headed toward him.

The boy managed to get out, and then a powerful explosion happened.

He survived, but lost his hearing.

Robon ended up under the debris and rocks.

But he was still alive.

## 2015 — The Return

A year later, Robon finally woke up.

His body was badly destroyed: he had lost one arm, one eye and both legs.

But the virus kept working.

His main goal became restoring his own body.

The chip discovered that Robon could use electrical cables to control mechanical parts.

He began searching for wires and connecting them to his body.

However, there wasn't enough electricity.

Then Robon found a desktop computer, took out its battery and connected it to his mechanical heart.

Energy started flowing again.

He collected about a thousand cables to restore his ability to move.

But his new arm couldn't handle the load and was badly damaged.

Soon he found another mechanical arm. It was a different color, but it fit him.

Now Robon could move again.

## What Happens Next?

Unknown.

Robon still exists. His virus keeps working.

And nobody knows what will happen when he finally restores his body...
